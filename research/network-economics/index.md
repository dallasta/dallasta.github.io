Networks provide the interaction structure through which individual decisions give rise to collective outcomes. My research in this area uses **statistical physics and network theory to study strategic interactions, coordination, control, and collective behaviour in networked systems**.

The underlying questions are closely related to those arising in statistical mechanics: how does local interaction produce global organization? How does network topology affect collective states? And how can individual incentives or external interventions reshape the macroscopic behaviour of a system?

<br>

## Coordination on Networks

Coordination problems provide a natural framework for studying how local interactions generate collective states. I have studied both static and dynamical aspects of coordination on networks, focusing on how network structure influences the emergence and stability of collective behaviour.

### Selected publications

- L. Dall’Asta, **Coordination problems on networks revisited: Statics and dynamics**, *Journal of Statistical Mechanics: Theory and Experiment* (2021).
- L. Dall’Asta, P. Pin & A. Ramezanpour, **Public Goods in Networks: A Statistical Mechanics Approach**, *Game Theory and Applications* (2013).
- L. Dall’Asta, P. Pin & A. Ramezanpour, **Optimal Equilibria of the Best Shot Game**, *Physical Review E* (2010).

<br>

## Strategic Interactions & Networked Systems

Strategic behaviour in a networked environment differs fundamentally from interactions in a well-mixed population. Local incentives, network topology, and the possibility of coordination or congestion can produce collective phenomena that are not apparent at the individual level.

This line of research connects game theory with statistical mechanics and complex-network theory.

### Selected publications

- F. Altarelli, A. Braunstein & L. Dall’Asta, **Statics and Dynamics of Selfish Interactions in Distributed Service Systems**, *PLOS ONE* (2015).
- L. Dall’Asta, P. Pin & A. Ramezanpour, **Public Goods in Networks: A Statistical Mechanics Approach** (2013).
- L. Dall’Asta, P. Pin & A. Ramezanpour, **Optimal Equilibria of the Best Shot Game** (2010).

<br>

## Network Control & Dismantling

The structure of a network determines its robustness, controllability, and response to targeted perturbations. My work in this direction has addressed the identification of critical structures and the development of statistical-mechanical approaches to network dismantling and control.

### Selected publications

- A. Braunstein, L. Dall’Asta, G. Semerjian & L. Zdeborová, **Network dismantling**, *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* (2016).
- G. Menichetti, L. Dall’Asta & G. Bianconi, **Control of Multilayer Networks**, *Scientific Reports* (2016).
- G. Menichetti, L. Dall’Asta & G. Bianconi, **Network Controllability Is Determined by the Density of Low In-Degree and Out-Degree Nodes**, *Physical Review Letters* (2014).

<br>

## Collective Behaviour on Networks

Networks can fundamentally change the nature of collective phenomena by introducing heterogeneous interaction patterns and spatial or topological constraints.

This broader perspective connects coordination and strategic interactions with the statistical physics of collective behaviour in complex networks.

### Selected publications

- L. Dall’Asta, **Coordination problems on networks revisited: Statics and dynamics**, *Journal of Statistical Mechanics: Theory and Experiment* (2021).
- A. Baronchelli, L. Dall’Asta, A. Barrat & V. Loreto, **Non-equilibrium phase transition in negotiation dynamics**, *Physical Review E* (2007).
- L. Dall’Asta, A. Baronchelli, A. Barrat & V. Loreto, **Agreement dynamics on small-world networks**, *Journal of Statistical Mechanics: Theory and Experiment* (2007).
