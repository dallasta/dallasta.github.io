# 🧪 Research Interests & Projects

## Main Research Tracks

### 1. Statistical Physics of Complex Networks
Non-equilibrium processes on networked structures, including epidemic modeling, information routing, and dynamical processes on hypergraphs and higher-order structures.

### 2. Message-Passing Algorithms & Graphical Models
Development of belief propagation, cavity methods, and message-passing techniques applied to constraint satisfaction problems, network inference, and optimization.

### 3. Computational Biology & Systems Biology
Applying methods from statistical mechanics to high-dimensional biological data, gene regulatory networks, and metabolic modeling.
