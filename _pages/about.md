I am an **Associate Professor of Theoretical Physics** at the Department of Applied Science and Technology ([DISAT](https://www.disat.polito.it/)) of **Politecnico di Torino**.

My research focuses on **statistical physics and complex systems**, with particular interests in collective phenomena, disordered systems, statistical inference, and non-equilibrium dynamics. I am interested in how concepts and methods from statistical mechanics can provide a unified framework for understanding complex interacting systems, from fundamental physical models to interdisciplinary applications.

<br>

## Research

My research combines analytical methods from statistical mechanics with computational approaches to study high-dimensional interacting systems, with applications ranging from complex networks and statistical inference to non-equilibrium and biological dynamics.

## Teaching

I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in **theoretical and statistical physics** at Politecnico di Torino. I supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses, as well as PhD and postdoctoral research projects, mainly on topics related to statistical mechanics, complex systems, stochastic processes and statistical inference.
