I am an **Associate Professor of Theoretical Physics** at the Department of Applied Science and Technology ([DISAT](https://www.disat.polito.it/)) of **Politecnico di Torino**.

My research is broadly concerned with **statistical physics and complex systems**, with a focus on theoretical approaches to collective phenomena, disordered systems, and non-equilibrium dynamics. I am particularly interested in problems where concepts and methods from statistical mechanics provide a powerful foundational language to understand complex interacting systems — ranging from fundamental physical models to interdisciplinary applications in network science, inference algorithms, and biological dynamics.

## Research Focus

My work combines analytical methods from non-equilibrium statistical mechanics with computational algorithms to tackle high-dimensional interacting systems. Core topics of interest include:

* **Disordered & Complex Systems:** Phase transitions, structural vulnerability, and localization phenomena on sparse random graphs and complex topographies.
* **Statistical Inference & Optimization:** Message-passing algorithms, cavity methods, and graphical models applied to inverse problems and data science.
* **Epidemic & Spreading Dynamics:** Modeling contagion processes, contact tracing, and optimal containment strategies on structured populations.
* **Stochastic Processes:** Anomalous diffusion, transport phenomena, and non-equilibrium steady states in interacting particle systems.
