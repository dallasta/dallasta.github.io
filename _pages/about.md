I am an Associate Professor of Theoretical Physics at the **Politecnico di Torino**.

My research is broadly concerned with **statistical physics and complex systems**, with a focus on theoretical approaches to collective phenomena, disordered systems and nonequilibrium dynamics.

I am interested in problems where concepts and methods from statistical mechanics provide a useful language for understanding complex interacting systems, from fundamental physical models to interdisciplinary applications.

## Research

My current research interests include:

- nonequilibrium statistical physics;
- disordered and complex systems;
- collective phenomena and dynamical processes;
- statistical-mechanical approaches to inference and optimization;
- applications of statistical physics to interdisciplinary problems.
