## 🔬 Overview
I am an Associate Professor in Theoretical Physics at Politecnico di Torino and a affiliate researcher at the [Italian Institute for Genomic Medicine (IIGM)](https://www.iigm.it/) and [Human Genetics Foundation (HuGeF)](https://www.hugef-torino.org/). 

My research lies at the intersection of **statistical mechanics**, **complex networks**, and **interdisciplinary applications of physics**, ranging from epidemic spreading and optimization on graphs to computational biology and inference algorithms.
